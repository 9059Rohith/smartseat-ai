# Production walkthrough

Recorded 5 November 2026 from https://smartseat-ai-9059.vercel.app/.

The approximately 2:33 MP4 uses actual browser screenshots sampled at about two frames per second. It is a chaptered product walkthrough with ordinary cuts between screens. No restaurant metrics, guest records, table zones, detections or cleaning confirmations were invented for recording. The camera reported a dark/covered view throughout. Original capture timing is retained within each chapter; time between chapters is omitted. Credentials, account chooser and account email are excluded; the bottom account-name strip is cropped from published frames. Chapter captions are separate from the application view.

The video demonstrates the floor/health display, guest and reservation forms, daily/weekend owner reports, CSV availability, real laptop source, table mapping form, actual monitoring/backup/restore/retention status and signed-out Google login screen. A real weekend CSV download was verified during the walkthrough. Forms were cancelled without adding fake restaurant records. The Google login was exercised after capture and the existing engine paired again.

This recording does not establish a visible-table occupancy/cleaning cycle, field precision/recall, SMTP acceptance or continuous hosting availability. The README and quality checklist retain those limitations. The separate DineAssist development clip is AI-generated and is not part of this production walkthrough.
